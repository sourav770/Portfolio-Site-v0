MY PROCESS

How Raw Footage Becomes Magic

A structured, high-efficiency workflow refined over 4+ years to deliver top-tier videos on schedule.

01

Ingestion & Storyboard

Organizing raw camera files, syncing multi-cam audio, tagging selects, and planning the narrative flow.

02

Rough Cut & Rhythm

Constructing the skeleton of the edit. Pacing cuts to music, building tension, and eliminating dead space.

03

Motion & VFX Polish

Adding kinetic typography, lower thirds, 3D element compositing, transitions, and dynamic visual effects.

04

Color & Sound Master

DaVinci Resolve color grading for filmic depth alongside multi-track sound design and audio cleaning.
