ABOUT ME

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT ME

Over the years, I've learned that good editing is more than cutting footage together — it's about knowing what to keep, what to remove, and when to make a moment matter. With 5+ years of hands-on experience and 2 years in corporate environments, I've worked across brands, agencies, businesses, and creators, bringing together storytelling, pacing, motion graphics, speed ramping, typography, color grading, and sound design. Whether it's a fast-paced reel, a branded campaign, a commercial, or a talking-head video, I build edits that are intentional, engaging, and made to communicate.
